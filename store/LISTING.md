## Description — English
Thingual is a playful way to learn a language, three minutes at a time.

A PATH FROM A1 TO C2
100 units and almost 500 lessons: meet new words with pictures and sound, match pairs, listen and build sentences, type answers. Each lesson opens the next one, with treasure chests along the way. Tingo the fox keeps you company.

16 LANGUAGES, 1,200+ WORDS, A1 TO C2
English, Ukrainian, Spanish, Portuguese, German, French, Italian, Polish, Turkish, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, Hindi, Indonesian and Vietnamese — with romanisation for non-Latin scripts. Browse 27 topics — from food and family to feelings, work and ideas — each word tagged with its level from A1 to C2, with beautiful 3D illustrations.

PHRASES FOR REAL LIFE
386 ready phrases for cafés, hotels, travel, the doctor and small talk in every language. For English learners: 282 phrasal verbs, 267 collocations and 142 idioms.

LEARN SMARTER
Spaced repetition (FSRS, the algorithm behind modern flashcards) schedules every review at the right moment. Seven exercise types, pronunciation feedback, 45 grammar lessons from A1 to C2, phrasal verbs and idioms, audiobooks read by native speakers, your own vocabulary for any word (with pictures), and an AI conversation partner for real dialogues.

STAY MOTIVATED
Hearts for mistakes, XP for every lesson, a daily streak with freezes and rewards, crystals for time spent learning and a weekly league with friends.

THREADS
Write in the language you learn — answer the topic of the day, ask a question, share a tradition. Other learners and native speakers reply and correct your mistakes right in the thread.

Private by design: no ads, no tracking, no location.

## Review notes (paste into App Review Information)
No login is required — the app creates an anonymous account automatically. To test community features, open the Threads tab and post a thread (or correct someone's post with Fix); you will be asked for an age group and to accept the community terms first. Objectionable content can be reported (⋯ on any post) and users can be blocked; photos are checked by an AI safety filter before publishing. Account deletion: Settings → Privacy → Delete account. Subscriptions (Thingual Max, monthly/yearly) are sold through the store via RevenueCat; Restore purchases is on the Max screen.
